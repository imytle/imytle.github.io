I am a junior in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST). My research interest is **hardware–software co-design for efficient LLM inference**, with a focus on low-bit quantization.

I am currently a research intern at the GIST System-level Memory, Processor, and Logic Engineering Lab, advised by Prof. Sangjin Kim, where I study block-scaled formats and vector quantization for LLM inference. Previously, I fabricated neuromorphic devices based on 2D materials at the GIST Nano Electronics & Optoelectronics Laboratory under Prof. Dongho Kang.

I am a recipient of the National Presidential Science Scholarship, and my team won 1st place in the GIST AX Challenge for designing a vision-guided ping-pong robot.
